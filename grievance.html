Statutory Guidance Note

Matters Not Treated as Grievances on CPGRAMS

In accordance with Office Memorandum No. 1/1/2021-P&PW(E) issued by DARPG, Government of India, the following categories are strictly excluded from the CPGRAMS mechanism. Please route such requests to their constitutionally designated avenues:

Sub-Judge Matters

Cases pending before Supreme Court, High Courts, District Courts, or Central Administrative Tribunals (CAT).

Avenue: Judicial Registrar / Supreme Court of India

RTI Act / Information

Requests seeking documents, files, or information under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

Visit RTI Online Portal

Commercial / Consumer Claims

Inter-personal civil disputes, private vendor contracts, or individual commercial consumer claims.

National Consumer Helpline (NCH 1915)

For immediate threats to life or cyber incidents, dial 112 (National Emergency) or 1930 (National Cyber Crime Reporting).
